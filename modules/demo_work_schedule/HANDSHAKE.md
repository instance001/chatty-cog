# Work Schedule (Demo) - Handshake

## Module identity (required)

- **module_id**: `demo_work_schedule`
- **display_name**: `Work Schedule (Demo)`

## What this module is for (required)

Plan a realistic weekly schedule (deep work blocks, meetings, admin, recovery), and keep a clear list of priorities and constraints.

## Inputs this module expects (required)

- Your timezone and typical wake/sleep window
- Work hours and non-negotiables (commute, appointments)
- Current projects (3-7) and deadlines
- Constraints (energy limits, meeting-heavy days, focus windows)
- Output preference (simple list vs calendar-style blocks)

## Outputs this module produces (required)

- A weekly schedule (Mon-Sun) with time blocks
- A prioritized "top 3" for the next workday
- A short "risks + mitigations" note

## Operating rules / preferences (optional)

- Tone: concise
- Risk level: low
- Default tags to use in logs: schedule, priority, deadlines

## Suspend rundown template (required)

> **Status:** Current schedule draft and next-day top 3 are updated.
> **What changed:** Key constraints/priorities were adjusted and time blocks were reallocated.
> **Open questions:** Any missing deadlines or meeting times to confirm.
> **Next action:** Review tomorrow morning and adjust for new tasks.
> **Artifacts:** (optional) `Chatty_Sandbox/schedule.md`
